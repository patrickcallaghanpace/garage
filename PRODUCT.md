# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Car owners in French-speaking Switzerland (FR default, EN available), usually on their phone, who need summer/winter tyres changed and would rather not spend an afternoon at a garage. They arrive from a Google Business Profile "Book" button or a link, with one job: get a tyre change booked at their own address, at a time that works, in under two minutes.

## Product Purpose

Pneu@Home is an independent mobile tyre-change service: a mechanic drives a trailer to the customer's home or workplace. The website exists to turn a visit into a confirmed appointment. The scheduling engine only offers slots that keep the mechanic's route efficient, so the page must make "pick one of these times" feel like a convenience, not a restriction. Success: a visitor books without calling, and trusts the business enough to do so.

## Brand Personality

Bold, no-nonsense, local. A trusted tradesperson, not a corporate brand: confident, direct, hands-on. The identity is the black-and-white wordmark (heavy italic, tyre sitting on a house roof) and the trailer livery (black tyre-track graphics on white). Voice: short, plain French, practical ("On vient chez vous"), no hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic blue-and-white car-service templates.
- Stock photos of smiling mechanics; any imagery that isn't the real trailer, the real tyres, the real region.
- Luxury, fussy, or "premium automotive" styling.
- Cookie-cutter SaaS landing patterns (identical icon-card grids, hero metrics, gradient text).

## Design Principles

1. **Booking is the point.** Every screen moves the visitor toward a confirmed slot; the primary action is always visible and obvious on a phone.
2. **Look like the trailer.** The site should feel like the livery: heavy black type, white space, tyre-track texture, real photography.
3. **Plain and quick.** Short copy, few fields, large tap targets; reassurance comes from clarity, not decoration.
4. **Local and real.** Use the real photo, real contact details, real service area; never stand-in content.
5. **Calm under the bold.** Strong identity, but forms and slot choices stay quiet, legible, and unambiguous.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the baseline: body text at least 4.5:1 contrast, visible focus states, tap targets of at least 44px, labels on every field, reduced-motion respected. French and English must both fit without overflow.
